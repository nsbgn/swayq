# contrib

This [contrib] directory contains some useful `swayq` scripts to serve 
as inspiration or example. They may be untested and no promises are made 
as to their interface or their continued availability.

They are also not built-in, so if you want to use any, you should copy 
them to your `~/.config/swayq/`. As scripts here mature and are deemed 
sufficiently generic, they may move to built-in.

[contrib]: https://drewdevault.com/2020/06/06/Add-a-contrib-directory.html
